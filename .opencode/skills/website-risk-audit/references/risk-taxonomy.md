# Risk Taxonomy

## Fraud / Scam
Signals:
- Guaranteed returns
- Urgent payment pressure
- Fake testimonials
- No company registration
- No refund policy
- Requests crypto, gift cards, wire transfer
- Fake recovery services
- Impersonation of government, bank, exchange, or brand

## Gambling
Signals:
- Casino
- Slots
- Sportsbook
- Betting
- Odds
- Roulette
- Blackjack
- Deposit bonus
- Wagering requirement
- Withdraw winnings
- No KYC gambling

## Adult / Pornographic
Signals:
- Porn
- XXX
- Adult videos
- Escort
- Webcam sexual services
- 18+ explicit content
- Nude content sales

## Phishing / Impersonation
Signals:
- Verify your account
- Suspended account
- Password reset
- Login immediately
- Credential collection
- Brand impersonation
- Mismatched domain and brand

## Suspicious Financial / Crypto
Signals:
- Guaranteed profit
- Double your money
- Risk-free investment
- Forex signal scheme
- Crypto recovery
- Unrealistic ROI
- No license disclosure
- Pressure to deposit quickly

## Malware / Suspicious Downloads
Signals:
- Fake browser update
- Download executable
- Security warning impersonation
- Obfuscated download links
- Cracked software
